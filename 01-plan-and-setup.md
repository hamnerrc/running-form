# Session plan and setup: retest + takeoffs (next session, ≈ mid-December 2026)

The baseline plan (2026-11-15) is in git history. This session repeats the baseline sprint setup exactly, so
the numbers are directly comparable, and adds **full takeoffs into the pit** filmed from a second camera
position. The full technical brief is in the [README](README.md); standard terms are defined in
[04](04-analysis-method.md#terms).

## 1. Field setup: identical to 2026-11-15

- **Zone:** 20-yard line → 30-yard line (10 yd = 9.144 m), same end of the field as last time.
- **Markers:** orange cones on the painted line through the 20 and the 30 (near row). Red bottle on the 20 and black cylinder on the 30, **3.0 m** further across (far row). Measure the 3.0 m again.
- **Camera:** tripod on the near sideline, square to the 25-yard line, **hip height (~1 m)**, same spot as last time. All markers, the 20/25/30 lines and the full runner must be in frame. **240 fps slo-mo, 1× lens (never 0.5×), lock focus and exposure** (long-press), never touch it between reps.
- **Wind flag (new, 1 minute):** tie a strip of tape or a ribbon to the top of the black cylinder so it is in frame. That shows the wind direction and strength on every rep.
- **Flying sprints:** same run-up as last time (start at least 30 m before the zone), flat out through the zone, run past the far markers before slowing.
- **Approach-run simulations:** use your **real full approach** (same number of steps from your normal checkmark). Place your start so that your **last step lands on the 30-yard line for → reps (20-yard line for ← reps)**, as if the board were there. Run through as if taking off, but don't jump. That puts the last 10 yd before the "board" in the zone.

## 2. Pit setup (new)

```
                 pit (sand)
   runway  ─────────────[board]═══════════  ← you run this way (either direction is fine)
    near edge  ●(−5 m)   ●(−2.5 m)   ●(0 = board front edge)
    far edge   ●(−5 m)   ●(−2.5 m)   ●(0)
                     📷 tripod ~10 m from the runway, level with ~2.5 m before the board
```

- **Camera:** on the **right side of the runway as you run toward the pit** (so your right, takeoff leg faces the camera). About **10 m** from the runway's centre line, **hip height (~1 m)**, square to the runway, centred about **2.5 m before the board**. In frame: from ~7 m before the board to ~2 m into the pit. 240 fps, 1×, lock focus/exposure.
- **Markers (≈ 5 minutes, needs a tape measure):** small pieces of the pink tape flat on the runway edges (or chalk), at:
  - the **board's front edge** (the edge nearest the pit), on both runway edges;
  - **2.5 m and 5.0 m before** the board's front edge, on both edges (6 marks in total).
  - Measure the **runway width** between the two edges (usually 1.22 m) and write it down.
- **Calibration clip:** 5 s of the empty runway with all markers visible, before the first jump. If the tripod moves, film another.
- **Each jump, write down:** the **official distance** (measured from the board's front edge to the nearest mark in the sand, as in competition), **foul or not**, and approach length (steps and metres). The video measures how far behind the board you took off.

## 3. Write down on the day (2 minutes)

- **Body mass** that morning (lb). Shoes (same spikes). Temperature.
- **Wind**: direction and strength for each rep (the flag in frame backs this up).
- **Each rep**: who, rep number, direction, how it felt (1–10 effort), any pain.
- Sleep the night before and soreness (0–10), for comparing sessions.

## 4. Easy extras that improve the analysis

- **What gets compared:** every stride is judged at the five key positions (toe-off, top of flight, strike, touchdown, full support) against world-class values (02/03). RC's retest targets: thigh separation at touchdown −34° → about −15° (swing knee level with the stance knee: the "figure 4"), push-off knee ≤ 160° at toe-off, knee lift → 72°+, approach speed → 19.6 mph. For a miler, film **2 kick-pace runs each way** as well as mile pace: the kick is where sprint mechanics matter.
- **Knees and hips matter most now.** If using tape, the side of the knee and the hip bone are the two most useful spots (the figure-4 measure depends on both knees).

- **Arms visible:** a fitted short-sleeve shirt, no jacket. The analysis now measures arm action (shoulder range of motion, elbow angle, hand height and how far the hands go behind the hips, and arm–leg timing).
- **Optional landmark tape** (pink, small squares) on the side facing the camera: outer ankle bone, side of the knee, hip bone, top of the shoulder, **elbow, wrist**. It improves joint accuracy; skip it if short on time.
- **Send the original 240 fps files**: AirDrop with Options → "All Photos Data" on. Last time the files were 30 fps renders of the slo-mo, which still worked but loses the timestamps.
