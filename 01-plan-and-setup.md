# Session plan and setup (baseline)

Both athletes use **the same setup**. The only difference is how far back each one starts and how fast he runs. The full technical brief, including what the analysis does with each file, is in the [README](README.md).

## Football field layout

```
 G1 goal line                       50          40 (G2 side)                G2 end line
   |---------- 50 yd (45.7 m) -------|== ZONE ==|---------- 40 yd + 10 yd end zone ----------|
   → start                           cone     cone                                  ← start
                                     cone     cone   (1.5 m across, measured)
                                         📷 sideline at the 45 (inside the zone)
```

- **Zone:** 50-yard line → the G2-side 40 = **10 yd (9.144 m)**, center of line to center of line.
- **Cones:** on the 50 and the 40, at the near hash and **1.5 m** further toward the middle. Measure that 1.5 m exactly.
- **Run-ups:** → reps start at the G1 goal line; ← reps start at the G2 end line. Both are 45.7 m before the zone. Starting closer is fine.
- **Camera:** tripod just behind the sideline at the 45, square to the running line, hip height. Use the sideline on the runner's **right** when running →. Sun not in the lens.
- **Settings:** slo-mo **240 fps** (1080p fine) · **1× lens, never 0.5×** · lock focus/exposure · never touch it between reps.
- Optional 2nd phone at the end of the running line, facing the runner, at shin height.
- **Send the original files** (AirDrop, or Share → Options → "All Photos Data").

## Before the session (indoors)
- **Athlete A tape measures**, both sides, barefoot: height · floor → hip bone point (trochanter) · hip point → side of knee joint · knee joint → outer ankle bone · floor → outer ankle bone · foot length.
- **Athlete A photos:** front / right side / back in A-pose from 3–4 m, camera at hip height, a known-length tape on the floor · one natural side photo · knee-to-wall photos from the side, both feet.
- Fill in `data/_template/session.yaml` and `measurements.yaml` (or just write the numbers down).

## Reps

**Landmark tape** on both sides: outer ankle bone, side of the knee joint, hip bone point, top of the shoulder (and shoe heel if possible). Colors that stand out against the clothes.

**Athlete A**
1. Calibration stand: stand still sideways in the middle of the zone for 3 s.
2. Film the warm-up strides at 75% and 90% through the zone.
3. **3 × flies: rep 1 →, rep 2 ←, rep 3 →.** 4–5 min full rest. Top speed by the zone, relaxed. Same shoes all session (trainers, no spikes).
4. *(optional, only if pain-free)* Pogo hops in the zone, side-on: 10 on both legs, then 5 per leg.
5. *(optional)* One standing broad jump in the zone, side-on.

**Athlete B**
1. Calibration stand (3 s, sideways, middle of the zone).
2. **3 × mile pace** through the zone (alternate directions if possible). Run through the far cones before slowing.
3. *(optional)* 1 × kick pace (last-200 m speed).
4. He's already tired from the meet, so no separate "tired" pass is needed.

**Targets through the 10 yd zone:** A: 9 m/s = 1.02 s, 8.5 m/s = 1.08 s. B: 5:15 pace = 1.79 s, 5:00 pace = 1.71 s.
