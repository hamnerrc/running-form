# Session plan and setup (baseline)

Both athletes use **the same setup**. The only difference is how far back each one starts and how fast he runs.

## Layout

```
Athlete A (sprint):  [build-up 20–30 m]──[fly 20 m]──[CAMERA ZONE 10 m]
Athlete B (mile):    [run-in ~50–60 m at mile pace]──[CAMERA ZONE 10 m]──[keep running ~10 m]
```

- **Camera zone:** exactly 10 m (or 10 yd between football yard lines — just record which).
- **4 markers** at the zone's corners: start and end, on both the near and far side of the running line, ~1–1.5 m apart. Record the across spacing exactly.
- Athlete B needs **no extra setup**: put one rough marker ~50–60 m before the zone so he can settle into pace, and have him run through the far end of the zone rather than slowing inside it.

### Measuring
- **Football field (best):** run lengthwise down the middle; zone = 10 yd between two yard lines (9.14 m). Camera on the sideline bleachers ≈ 20–25 m away.
- **Grass, no lines:** a string or rope measured to exactly 10 m at home. Across spacing: a tape, or a string cut to 1.5 m.
- Build-up and run-in distances can be paced off; only the zone and the across spacing must be exact.

## Camera
- Tripod (borrowed) **15–20 m** to the side, square to the zone's midpoint, at hip height.
- Zoom so all 4 markers and the full body fit in the frame. Don't move or re-zoom between reps.
- **Slo-mo 240 fps** (120 at minimum), focus/exposure locked.
- Optional 2nd phone at the end of the line facing the runner, at shin height (shows ankles rolling in or out; most useful for Athlete A's right ankle).
- **Send the original files** (AirDrop, or Share → Options → "All Photos Data"). Re-encoded files often drop to 30 fps.

## Reps

**Athlete A — 3 × 30 m flies**, 4–5 min full rest between reps. Top speed by the start of the fly, relaxed. Same shoes all session (trainers; no spikes this phase).

**Athlete B — 4 passes, at most 6** (the last two are optional):
1. **3 × mile race pace** through the zone (more passes → more steps to average; ~6 steps per pass). Rest as needed.
2. *(optional)* **1 × kick pace** (last-200 m speed). Mile races are often decided by speed, and elite 1500 m research suggests the best runners use a speed-oriented technique (see [03](03-reference-miler.md)).
3. *(optional)* **1 × mile pace when tired** (right after a hard 400–800 m) to see how his form changes with fatigue.

Target times through the 10 m zone, so he can sanity-check pace:

| Mile time | Speed | 10 m time |
|---|---|---|
| 4:30 | 5.96 m/s | 1.68 s |
| 5:00 | 5.36 m/s | 1.86 s |
| 5:30 | 4.88 m/s | 2.05 s |
| 6:00 | 4.47 m/s | 2.24 s |

## Info needed
- **Athlete A:** have everything (height 5'9", PR 19'3", right-foot takeoff, injury notes below).
- **Athlete B:** 5’10” 5:15 estimated mile PR right now, sub 5 goal, will be very tired day of filming from meet the day before. (good anyways to test form under fatigue). shin splints.
